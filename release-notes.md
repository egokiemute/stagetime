## Messages the speaker can't miss
- Stage messages now fill the whole screen in large, bold text, covering the time, so the speaker can see them from anywhere on the platform.
- A message leaves by itself after 10 seconds and the timer comes back. Click the button again to send it again.
- Press **Clear message** or Esc, or click the highlighted button again, to remove a message early.
- The control window and phones show how long the message has left, such as "On stage · 7s".

## See which screen you're connected to
- Once the stage window is showing, **Send to screen** turns green and names the screen, such as "On LG ULTRAFINE".
- Click it to move the stage window to a different screen.
