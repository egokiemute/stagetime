What it does

  • Two windows, one app — a control panel on your Mac, a full-screen countdown on the projector
  • Never quietly wrong: both screens always show the same number, accurate to the second over a 45-minute service
  • Overrun is unmissable — the countdown turns red before zero, then flips to a slow-breathing red "Time Up" screen
  • Paste a service order from WhatsApp and step through it segment by segment, with drift and projected finish time tracked live
  • Send messages to the platform ("5 minutes left," "Time is up") without touching the countdown
  • Keeps the Mac from sleeping or dimming for the whole service
